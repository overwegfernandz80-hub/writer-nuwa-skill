# Writer Nuwa Promo Assets

Core message: **HOW THEY WRITE, not WHAT THEY WROTE.**

Do not use promotional assets to imply direct copying, quotation collage, or complete recreation of an author's voice. Living-author promotion should emphasize analysis, writing education, and high-level mechanisms.
